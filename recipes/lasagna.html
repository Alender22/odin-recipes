This should be a simple recipe to easily cook Lasagna. As I am no cook, I am following these instructions for how to cook lasagna in this recipe page.

ingredients:

    Cheese Spread

  • 10 oz. rricotta chese, 2 cups.
  • 1 large egg.
  • 2 cups mozarella cheese.
  • 3/4 cup Parmesan cheese, freshly grated.
  • 2 teaspoons italian seasoning.
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt.
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper.

    • Meat Sauce

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil.
  • 1 yellow onion, finely diced.
  • 3/4 lb. ground beef.
  • 3/4 lb. italian sausage.
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced.
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth.
  • 40 oz. marinara sauce.
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste.
  • 1 teaspoon hot sauce.
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce.

    • Lasagna Noodles/ Cheese Topping

  • 12 lasagna noodleas, plus extra in case of breakage.
  • 2.5 cups mozarella cheese.

cooking steps:

    Prep work

  1. Combine cheese spread ingredients in medium bowl and set aside.

    2. Make the Meat sauce

  2. Heat olive oil over medium heat.
  3. Add the diced onions.
  4. Let them soften for ten minutes.
  5. Add the ground beef and sausage.
  6. Increase heat to medium-high.
  7. Use a spatula to break up the meat so that it's very fine and crumbled.
  8. Cook for 8-10 minutes, or until cooked through.
  9. Add garlic during the last minute.
  10. Drain excess grease.
  11. Add the chicken broth.
  12. Use a silicone spatula to clean the bottom and sides of the pot.
  13. Add the marinara sauce, tomato paste, hot sauce, and Worcestershire sauce.
  14. Cover partially and let the sauce simmer while you boil the noodles.

    15. Preheat Oven and Cook the Lasagna Noodles

  15. Preheat oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit.
  16. Begin boiling large pot of salted pasta water.
  17. Once rapid boiling is reached, cook the noodles to al dente.
  18. Gently stir with wooden spoon throughout cooking to keep the noodles from sticking.
  19. Lay the cooled noodles flat on wax parchment paper while you begin assembling the Lasagna.

    20. Assemble

  20. Spread 1 heaping cup of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13 inch casserole dish.
  21. Add 4 lasagna noodles, overlapping them if needed.
  22. Spread 1/3 of the cheese spread mixture over the noodles.
  23. Spread 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce next.
  24. Add 4 more lasagna noodles, overlapping them if needed.
  25. Spread 1/3 of the cheese spread mixture over the noodles.
  26. Spread 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce next.
  27. Add 4 more lasagna noodles, overlapping them if needed.
  28. Spread remaining cheese spread mixture over the noodles.
  29. Spread remaining meat sauce.
  30. Top with 2 1/2 cups of mozzarella cheese.
  31. Spray foil with nonstick cookikng spray and place it spray-side-down on top of the baking dish to prevent the cheese from sticking to it.

    32. Bake

  32. Bake for 35 minutes.
  33. Remove cover and bake for additional 10 minutes.
  34. Remove from oven and garnish with fresh parsley.
  35. Let it sit for 15 minutes prior to serving, to allow the layers to set.
