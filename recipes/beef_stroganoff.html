Beef Stroganoff

home

This should be a simple recipe to easily cook beef stroganoff. As I am no cook, I am following these instructions for how to cook beef stroganoff in this recipe page.

ingredients:

  • 600g scotch fillet steak/boneless rib eye
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 300g mushrooms
  • 40g butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 500 ml beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 150 ml sour cream
  • salt and pepper

cooking steps:

  1. Flatten steak to 3/4 cm thickness (use fist or rolling pin).
  2. Cut into 5mm wide strips. Cut long strips in half.
  3. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Add tbsp of oil to skillet and heat skillet.
  5. Spread 1tbsp oil in skillet. For half the strips quickly sear both sides for 30s.
  6. Add tbsp of oil to hot skillet.
  7. Sear the remaining strips for 30s per side in hot skillet.
  8. Turn heat down to medium high.
  9. Add butter and let it melt.
  10. Add onions and let it cook for one minute.
  11. Add mushrooms and cook until golden brown. Make sure to scrape bottom of pan to for increased flavor.
  12. Add flour. Cook for one minute while stirring.
  13. Slowly add broth while stirring.
  14. Add sour cream and mustard. Stir until incorporated.
  15. Bring to simmer, then reduce heat to medium low.
  16. Wait for it to reach the consistence of pouring cream. Then adjust salt and pepper by taste.
  17. Add in the seared beef bits and let simmer for one minute then immediately remove from stove.
  18. Serve over pasta or egg noodles.
