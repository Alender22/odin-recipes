Macaronee And Cheese

home

This should be a simple recipe to easily cook beef stroganoff. As I am no cook, I am following these instructions for how to cook baked macaroni and cheese in this recipe page.

ingredients:

  • 1 lb. dried elbow pasta.
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter.
  • 1/2 cup all purpose flower.
  • 1 1/2 cups whole milk.
  • 2 1/2 cups half and half.
  • 4 cups shredded medium cheddar cheese.
  • 2 cups shredded Gruyere cheese.
  • 1/2 Tbsp. salt.
  • 1/2 tsp. black pepper.
  • 1/4 tsp. paprika.

cooking steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F.
  2. Grease a 3 qt baking dish (9x13 inches).
  3. Bring a alrge pot of salted water to a boil.
  4. Add pasta and cook for 1 minute less than package indicates for al dante.
  5. Drain and drizzle with a little bit of olive oil to keep from sticking.
  6. While water is comming up to a boil, shred cheeses and toss together to mix, then divide into three piles. Approximately 3 cups for the sauce, 1 1/2 cups for the inner layer, and 1 1/2 cups for the topping.
  7. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
  8. Sprinkle in flour and whisk to combine.
  9. Cook for about one minute, whisking often.
  10. Slowly pour in about 2 cups of the half and half whil constantly whisking.
  11. Whisk until smooth.
  12. Slowly pour in the remaining half and half and the whole milk while constantly whisking.
  13. Whisk until combined and smooth.
  14. Continue to heat over medium heat whisking often. Continue until it reaches a very thick consistency.
  15. Remove from heat and stir in the spices and 1 1/2 cups of the cheeses, stirring to melt and combine.
  16. Stir in another 1 1/2 cups of cheese and stir until completely melted and smooth.
  17. In a large mixing bowl combine drained pasta with cheese sauce, stirring to combine fully.
  18. Pour half of the pasta mixture into the preapred baking dish.
  19. Top with 1 1/2 cups of shredded cheeses, then top that with the remaining pasta mixture.
  20. Sprinkle the top wit hthe last 1 1/2 cups of cheese and bake for 15 minutes, until the cheese is bubbly and lightly golden brown.
